    How it works

    1. You choose the gift

      Pick a stock token and an amount. It's held safely by the gift contract, not by us.

    2. You send a link

      Text it, email it, or tuck it in a card. The link is the gift, so keep it private.

    3. They claim it

      They open the link and choose a wallet. Unclaimed gifts return to you after they expire.
