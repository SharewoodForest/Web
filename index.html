Questions, answered

What is Sharewood Forest?

Sharewood Forest is a web app for giving tokens, tokenized stocks and ETH as a gift. You choose what to give, write a short dedication, and seal it into a smart contract. You get a private link to send to the person it's for.

How does the person open their gift?

They open the link, connect any Ethereum-compatible wallet and tap Open gift. The contents and a keepsake gift certificate go to their wallet. If the sender covered the network fee, opening is free for them.

Do I need to copy or paste any wallet details?

No. You connect your wallet and approve each step inside your wallet app. Sharewood Forest never asks for your seed phrase or wallet private key, and you don't paste a recipient address. An optional advanced control lets you add a token by its contract address if it isn't listed.

What happens if a gift isn't opened?

Every gift has an expiry that you choose, from 7 days to 1 year. After it expires, the sender can take the gift back from the same page.

Is the gift link private?

The key that opens a gift sits after the # in the link, so browsers never send it to any server. Anyone who has the whole link can open the gift, so share it only with the person it's for.

Which networks does it support?

Robinhood Chain, Base and Arbitrum, plus test networks for trying it out. The network menu in the app shows what is live. The contract source is public at github.com/SharewoodForest/Contracts.

Is Sharewood Forest affiliated with Robinhood?

No. It is an independent app built on Robinhood Chain and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Robinhood. Stock tokens are not available everywhere, so check that gifting and receiving them is allowed where you and your recipient live. Nothing here is financial advice.
